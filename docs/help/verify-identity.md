# Verify your identity

Everything happens on **My Identity** in the left menu. The **Verification progress** card shows each step and ticks it off when it's done. Do the steps in this order — the NIN check only unlocks after the face check.

## 1. Liveness check (face check)

A short camera check that proves a real, live person is there — not a photo or a screen.

1. Under **Liveness check**, press **Verify now**.
2. Choose **Use this device**, or **Continue on my phone** to scan a QR code and use your phone's camera instead (no need to log in on the phone; the link lasts 10 minutes and works once).
3. Allow camera access when your browser asks.
4. Follow the prompts — for example **Turn your head to your left**, **Turn your head to your right**, **Open your mouth wide**.
5. When it says **Liveness confirmed**, you're done.

**Tips:** find good, even light on your face; remove sunglasses or hats; make sure only you are in view.

**If it fails:** the message tells you why — *Your face left the camera view*, *More than one face was in view*, *The image looked like a photo or screen*, or *The check took too long*. Just try again.

FOID stores your face template encrypted, so it can confirm it's you later (for example the first time you share with a new business). You can delete it in **Profile & Settings** → **Linked identity documents** → **Face (liveness)** → **Delete** — you'll need a new face check afterwards.

## 2. Verify NIN

1. Under **Verify NIN**, press **Verify now**.
2. Enter your **First name**, **Middle name** (optional), **Last name** and **Date of birth** exactly as they appear on your NIN record.
3. Press **Verify NIN**.

FOID checks them against the national record. Once verified, your name and date of birth are locked to the NIN record.

## 3. Verify BVN

Under **Verify BVN**, enter your 11-digit BVN and press **Verify now**. Only digits are accepted.

## 4. Phone number

Under **Verify phone number**, enter your phone number and press **Add number** to add it to your profile.

## What "verified" means

When the steps are done, your FOID credential is **active**: businesses you approve can rely on the confirmations without asking you to repeat KYC.

Your credential can change state, and FOID **always notifies you** when it does, with the reason:

| State | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Active** | In good standing. |
| **Under review** | Something needs a closer look (for example, a change in activity on a bank account you connected). Nothing is taken away yet. |
| **Downgraded** | Part of your credential is no longer confirmed. You'll see what changed and what you can do. |
| **Revoked** | The credential is not valid. |

FOID never gives you a credit score and never decides whether you get a loan — that decision always stays with the business.

## Your identity summary

On **My Identity** or **Verification Credentials**, **Download Identity Summary** gives you a PDF of what you've verified.
