# Create your FOID

You need an email address you can open and your 11-digit NIN.

## Sign up

1. Open FOID and choose **Create your FOID** (on the log-in page), or go straight to the sign-up page.
2. Fill in **First name**, **Middle name** (optional), **Last name**, your **NIN**, **Email address**, **Password** and **Confirm Password**.
   - Use your names **exactly as they appear on your NIN** — FOID checks them against the NIN record later.
   - As you type your password, a checklist shows what's still missing. See [password rules](./security#your-password).
3. Accept the terms and press **Sign Up**.

You land on your dashboard straight away.

## Confirm your email

Until you confirm your email, a purple bar at the top says **"Your email is not verified — you are in view-only mode."**

1. Open the email from FOID and copy the 6-digit code.
2. Press **Verify** on the purple bar, enter the code and press **Verify Email**.
3. You'll see **Email verified!** — your account is now fully active.

Didn't get it? Check spam, then press **Resend code** in the same window. The code expires after 30 minutes.

::: warning
Accounts whose email isn't confirmed are **deleted automatically after 3 days**.
:::

## Next step

Go to **My Identity** and [verify your identity](./verify-identity). That's what lets you share a verified FOID with banks and apps.

## Logging in later

Use **Log In** with your email and password. You can also turn on [fingerprint or face sign-in](./security#fingerprint-or-face-sign-in-biometric-unlock) and [two-factor authentication](./security#two-factor-authentication).

Forgot your password? Choose **Forgot password** on the log-in page, enter your email, press **Send reset code**, then enter the code and your new password.
