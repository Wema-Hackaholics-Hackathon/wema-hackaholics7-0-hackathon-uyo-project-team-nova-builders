# Your FOID code

Your FOID code lets someone — a bank teller, a landlord, a shop — check that you're verified **without you reading out your NIN or BVN**.

## Show your code

1. Go to **Overview** and press **View my FOID code**.
2. A QR code and a link appear. Let the other person scan the QR code, or press **Copy link** and send it.
3. The code **lasts 10 minutes**. When it says **Expired**, open it again to get a fresh one.

## What the other person sees

A **Credential check** page with:

- your first name and the first letter of your last name (for example *Ada O.*),
- your credential state (**Active**, **Under review**, **Downgraded** or **Revoked**) and what it means,
- ticks for **Face (liveness) verified**, **NIN verified**, **BVN verified**, **Email confirmed**, and **Over 18** (only when your NIN is verified — your date of birth itself is never shown),
- the time of the check.

They **never** see your NIN, BVN, date of birth, email, phone or address.

## Is it safe to share?

Yes, for the 10 minutes it's valid. After that the link stops working (*"This FOID code has expired or isn't valid. Ask for a fresh one."*). If you think someone kept it, simply wait — it expires on its own.
