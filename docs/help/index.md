# Help centre

FOID is your verified identity, built once and reused. You prove who you are one time — your NIN, BVN and a quick face check — and then share that proof with banks and apps **only when you say yes**. You can see who has access and take it back at any time.

## Getting started

- [Create your FOID](./getting-started) — sign up and confirm your email
- [Verify your identity](./verify-identity) — face check, NIN, BVN and phone
- [Your FOID code](./foid-code) — show someone you're verified without reading out your NIN

## Sharing with businesses

- [Continue with FOID](./continue-with-foid) — signing in to a bank or app with FOID
- [Connected apps and consent](./connected-apps) — see who has access and revoke it

## Your account

- [Keep your account safe](./security) — password, two-factor authentication, fingerprint/face sign-in, login alerts
- [FAQ](./faq) — quick answers

::: tip What businesses see
Businesses get **confirmations** — "NIN verified", "BVN verified", "face check passed" — never your full NIN or BVN. They only see what you approved on the consent screen.
:::
