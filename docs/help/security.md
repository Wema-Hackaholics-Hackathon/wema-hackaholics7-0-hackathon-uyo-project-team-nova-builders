# Keep your account safe

All of these are in **Profile & Settings** → **Security**.

## Your password

Your password needs **at least 8 characters**, with an **uppercase letter**, a **lowercase letter**, a **number** and a **special character** (like `!` or `#`). A checklist shows each rule as you type, and the button stays off until every rule passes and both passwords match.

**Change it:** **Profile & Settings** → **Change password** → enter your **Current password**, a **New password** and **Confirm new**, then **Update password**.

**Forgot it:** on the log-in page choose **Forgot password**, press **Send reset code**, enter the code from your email, then set a new password.

## Two-factor authentication

Adds a second step when you log in: a 6-digit code from an authenticator app (Google Authenticator, Authy, Microsoft Authenticator, …).

**Turn it on**

1. Switch on **Two-factor authentication**.
2. Scan the QR code with your authenticator app.
3. Enter the 6-digit code the app shows and press **Turn on**.

From then on, after your password you'll see **Authenticator code** — open the app and enter the current code, then **Verify and log in**.

**Turn it off:** switch it off, then confirm with your **Password** and a **Code from your app**.

::: warning Keep your phone safe
If you lose the phone with your authenticator app, you can't complete the second step. Turn two-factor off before you change phones, then set it up again on the new one.
:::

## Fingerprint or face sign-in (Biometric unlock)

Log in with Face ID, your fingerprint or Windows Hello instead of typing your password — on **this device**.

1. Switch on **Biometric unlock**.
2. Confirm with your fingerprint, face or device PIN when your device asks.

Next time, press **Log in with fingerprint or face** on the log-in page. If it says *"Not available — this device has no fingerprint or face unlock"*, your device doesn't support it.

Your fingerprint or face never leaves your device — FOID only stores a key your device creates. Turning **Biometric unlock** off removes it.

## Login alerts

With **Login alerts** on (the default), FOID emails you and adds a notification when someone signs in to your account from a **new device**. The first device you use is remembered, so you won't get an alert for it.

Got an alert you don't recognise? **Change your password straight away** and turn on two-factor authentication.

## Face data

Your face template is stored encrypted. To delete it: **Profile & Settings** → **Linked identity documents** → **Face (liveness)** → **Delete**. You'll need a new liveness check on **My Identity** before you can share a verified FOID again.
